A bit more lore

I'm Munia, but you can call me Muns. I'm a designer who's equal parts curious and stubborn, most at home in UI/UX and the research behind it: getting to know people, asking the why a few too many times, and shaping something that genuinely fits them.

I like building what I design, so I'm picking up frontend, and I'm deep into machine learning & deep learning on the side, computer vision most of all. I can't claim I've mastered any of it, because there's always something more to learn, and honestly that's my favourite part.

Off the clock I'm cooking something new, lost in an anime or K-drama, and losing a ranked game in League of Legends. If you've got an idea worth chasing, let's build something meaningful (and a little fun) together.
