01 — Overview & Problem

The problem

The agency hired me to design their website from the ground up — one that attracts clients and communicates its services clearly, balancing creativity with professionalism across every device.

With no existing site to build on, the brief was to make a broad range of services instantly clear and give visitors an obvious reason — and way — to get in touch.

01

Several services to present — they had to be instantly scannable, not overwhelming.

02

Visitors needed an obvious, repeated path to get in touch.

03

It had to feel modern and work flawlessly across every device for a global audience.
