01 — The Product

What Craving is

Most of us already photograph our food — Craving turns that habit into something useful. It’s a feed of real meals from real people, a way to discover where to eat next, and a loop that pays you back for showing up.

I designed the UI and user flow and built the front-end, developing it as a full-stack product together with a friend. The brief I set: keep it familiar enough to feel instant — a clean feed, explore, reels and profile — but warm and appetite-first, so the food is always the hero. It’s live and still being shipped.

02 — Three Things It Does

Share, discover, earn

The whole product hangs on one simple loop — you share the food you eat, that helps other people find good spots, and restaurants reward you for both.

♢

Share

Post the meals you actually eat. A clean, food-first feed and reels — no clutter, just the plate.

⚲

Discover

Explore new spots through people whose taste you trust, instead of generic five-star noise.

★

Earn

Get rewarded by restaurants for showing up and sharing — the loop that keeps the feed honest.

03 — Where It’s At

In production

Craving is a live, in-progress product — not a finished retrospective. The core experience is up and shipping, and the design keeps evolving screen by screen as my friend and I build it out.

Build what you design

Designing the UI and writing the front-end myself kept the gap between intent and what actually ships almost zero.

Keep the food the hero

Every layout choice defers to the photo on the plate. Appetite first, interface second.
